# Natural Attestation rhythm in Cycle-entry space

## Result

No first Settlement → Emergence witness was found in the bounded matrix.
Fourteen histories (seven policies in each of two fixed-population panels) ran
100 genuine ParticipationService actions apiece. They executed **zero ordinary
Commits**. Five cases reached prospective Commit evaluations, all with negative
Tension. This is not a proof that no lawful history can cross, nor proof that the
candidate developmental Cadence formula is insufficient.

The candidate can form real regularity without idle population: the three-author
sparse policy produced a lineage with `[5,5]`, CV 0 and Cadence +1 at Phase 2.
The solo regular-1 policy produced `[1,1]` and positive existing-Gate Tension
`+0.0660794` at Phase 4 / Cycle 5. Neither observation is a Commit boundary.

## Method and controls

Base: `c83a48b441f08177f60c7e85968ce0f703af11dd`, the existing Origin-calibration /
formation diagnostic branch. The new candidate class differs from that formation
class only by namespace and extra trace fields. Origin empirical baseline 0,
Origin numeric Chirality -0.5, signed-displacement Orientation, the first-rhythm
transform, maturity handling, and later comparative Cadence remain as found.

Each panel fixes its population at one or three authors, all entering before
the first ordinary Attestation. No additional people or manually inserted Cycles
are created. Cycles thereafter arise only from ordinary participation. No
Rest-only empty-Cycle padding is permitted. The scheduler never reads a measured
field value to choose an action or adjust a coefficient.

The original Write/Traverse priority is held fixed. Requested gaps control when
the scheduler permits an available Attestation relative to other genuine work.
If no other lawful work can advance the spacing, it records the shortfall and
performs the available Attestation. Instrument requests are bounded to one per
Phase. Actual per-lineage represented intervals, not global scheduling targets,
feed the candidate calculator. Occurrence observers preserve fresh-run ordering;
wall-clock timestamps are evidence only, not the interval unit.

Production topology construction, effective evidence, Bias gating, Closure,
Correspondence, inverse-square ancestry attenuation, projection, and transition
services remain unchanged. The repository's existing deterministic hyperlink
verifier supplies test evidence; this is not a field/measurement mock or live
network evidence. Action availability can cause topology, signs, and Phase paths
to diverge between policies. Consequently this is a lawful scheduling experiment,
not a perfectly matched topology/evidence intervention or universal minimality proof.

## Why no actual Commit at the closest boundary?

A focused repeat reproduced all three regular-3 prospective rows numerically
and inspected the real plan. The explicit reason was **missing_effective_attestation**
on the Commit source. The requirement flags were:

| Requirement | Passes |

| Receipt | Yes |
| Closure | Yes |

Order offered only `participant.rest`. The aggregate lineage lean of +1 does
not establish effective Attestation on the specific Commit source. The negative
prospective field and missing source grounding are separate findings. No missing
Attestation was inserted directly, and no grounding gate was relaxed.

## Interpretation and surprises

- Natural first-Domain regularity is observable with fixed genuine population.
  The old idle-participant `[5,5,…]` crossover is excluded as a lawful witness.
- Positive Tension on an expressive Gate does not establish a Domain transition.
  A legal ordinary Commit remains necessary.
- A requested global spacing is not necessarily the observed lineage spacing:
  the lineage filters both represented entries and Attestation acts. Several
  policies coincide, and others fail to sustain their target pattern.
- The solo noisy case reached a late mature Bias disagreement (Orientation
  Positive, Cadence Negative). Several other histories had component agreement
  but only Rest available: component agreement is not the whole Write contract.
- Developmental Cadence has a demonstrated positive contribution, but this matrix
  has not established a grounded first-Commit crossover or earned canonical
  promotion. It would be unjustified to compensate by changing other measurements.
- The 100-action bound and selected scheduler do not exhaust all lawful histories,
  populations, or cooperative ways of grounding a Commit source. No universal
  earliest-history claim or impossibility result is made.

| Intervals | Unguarded CV calculation | Existing formation handling | Production Closure CV helper |
|---|---|---|---|

| `[2,2]` | CV 0 | Mature | CV 0 |
| `[0,1]` | CV 1 | Mature | DomainException |

Zero Cycle entries are valid Attestation intervals, whereas Closure's helper
requires at least two strictly positive intervals. Its guard therefore cannot
be imported unchanged. Semantic options for an all-zero mature history include
retaining undefined/unavailable status until positive progression is observed,
or explicitly defining a separate convention for coincident acts. No convention
was chosen or implemented. Dropping zero intervals would change the primitive.

Production still lacks the durable total ordering between Cycle entry and
Attestation occurrence that this fresh-run instrumentation supplies.
