blocked forward boundaries. At its first mature Constrain observation, the
existing Gate has Tension **+0.1108812** but no effective source Attestation.

## Exact blockers

### First failed forward step: step 6, Constrain

The acting Gate is the Persist Write authored at step 3. Completed source evidence
and authorized receipts exist. Its position is correct, it is expressive, and no
classification in the target register exists. But:

```php
PropositionStateService::attestations(collect([$gate]))->isEmpty() === true
```

Consequently `PhaseGenerationService::available(..., 'proposition.reclassify')`
returns false. This is the concrete `missing_effective_attestation` condition.
There are no current Constrain Writes to instrument, attest or traverse, and no
revision option. The actual schema contains only `participant.rest`.
The informational Chirality component disagreement at this early point is **not**
a Constrain prerequisite; it must not be reported as the cause of this rejection.

The original scheduler selected Instruments at Persist when Attestation was also
available. Those ordinary acts advanced participants into Constrain without a
vote on its source. This is an observed consequence of the preserved authored
sequence, not proof that no different lawful schedule could ground it.

Rest and subsequent ordinary Cycles continue. At step 33 the same rejection occurs
with mature Cadence and positive field. At step 48 A2 genuinely attests the Persist
Write; step 49 can then Reclassify. No diagnostic repair is involved.

### Mediate: steps 55, 57, 65

Correspondence directions already agree. The only available Gate is the acting
Gate itself; there is no distinct Reconcile operand. `ReconciliationOptionService`
excludes the current Gate from the partner list, and an empty target enum removes
Reconcile. At step 65 source grounding is also satisfied, proving missing
Attestation is not the explanation for that Mediate block. The ordinary Variant
at step 66 supplies a second Gate; Reconcile becomes available at step 69.

### First Bias: step 72 (also 73)

The source created by Reconcile at step 69 is regionally eligible, is the sole
operational Bias source, has matching Positive lineage-evidence/Chirality
directions, and its Orientation/Cadence directions agree Positive/Positive.
It has the correct Phase position, no prior classification, authorized ordinary

Yet **effective source Attestation count is zero**. The same generation predicate
rejects Reclassify; Bias has no causal Write yet to instrument, attest or traverse.
The schema again contains only Rest. No Order Phase or Commit candidate is reached.

Ancestral Attestations contribute to rhythm and lean, but do not become Attestations
on a newly reconciled expressive proposition. `PropositionStateService::sourceIds`

fails at source grounding, not at Chirality direction agreement, receipt availability,
Closure, Order or negative Tension.

Later Bias sources at 97/98 also lack direct evidence receipts. Ordinary authorized
classification receipts remain available; these are a different scope from completed
evidence on the new source. Step 97 additionally has negative existing-Gate Tension.

## Interpretation

- **B — lifecycle/admissibility:** directly demonstrated on the mature `[5,5]`
  Gate and at first Bias. Positive existing-Gate field is insufficient to license
  the next authored write. The Mediate partner requirement is another independent
  lifecycle condition.
- **D — mixed:** applies across the complete evidence. Before maturity, and at
  later primary step 97, negative field coexists with missing source evidence.
  The solo specimen directly demonstrates both negative prospective Commit field
  and a separate Commit-eligibility failure.
- **C — developmental sequencing:** not supported as the decisive explanation.
  Maturity does arrive after an earlier failed Constrain evaluation, but subsequent
  evaluations consume the mature measurement and are still blocked. The first Bias
  evaluation is already mature. No one-shot missed Cadence window is observed.
- The primary specimen does **not** prove field sufficiency for a lawful prospective
  Commit. It proves positive expressive measurements can coexist with blocked writes.
  A legal first-Commit crossover remains unestablished.

The central surprise is the coexistence of coherent **ancestral rhythm** and an
**unattested current source**. Another is that Reconcile changes which Attestation
acts belong to a lineage: `[5,5,5]` at one Mediate operand becomes `[5,5,5,2]` at its
result; later different reconciliations expose different interval lists. Cadence is
not a single global clock, nor a maturity flag that supplies source grounding.
