This is an observation of the same fixed three-participant history, under the unchanged provisional developmental Cadence diagnostic. It is not a new production rule or a proof that every alternative lawful history has the same outcome.

## Concrete structural dependency and the actual path

Bias consumes a **Mediate-produced** Stance/Frontier. A Persist-produced Stance cannot be promoted by positive Tension, grounding, matching register, another participant or a new Cycle. The forward dependency from P1 is:

1. Constrain Reclassify P1 → Q1.
2. Disperse Write from Q1 → S1.
3. Mediate Reconcile with a **distinct eligible Disperse partner** → R.
4. Only then is the participant at Bias, with R as the causal Phase-5 Gate.

At the positive snapshot there is no Constrain result, Disperse result, partner or Mediate result. At the first Mediate encounter S1 has no other eligible operand; a self-pair is excluded. The preserved participants then develop the second branch D2 → P2 → Q2 → S2. Reconcile(S2, S1) at **c18** creates R and opens Bias for A0, Cycle 7.

[ACTION-PATH.md](ACTION-PATH.md) records **every c1–c18 contribution**, both current fields, target/result, phase change and created record counts. This is the shortest **prefix of the preserved schedule** reaching Bias: 18 actions after c0, creating seven propositions, six Attestations, two Receipts and two Cycles. It is not a claim that all peer interleavings are logically mandatory or that no shorter alternative schedule exists. No alternative history was constructed.

## Interpretation and bounds

The initial positive window is real but structurally early. The next forward source loses positivity and current-source grounding before additional zero intervals appear. Same-entry Attestations then cause a separate direction loss. Finally, Reconcile creates another fresh source whose grounding must be authored again. Thus the mechanism is **structural sequencing plus changing field geometry, with independent fresh-source grounding and later Cadence deterioration**. It is not solely a Cadence window being consumed by necessary preparatory votes.

The source explicitly checks the current post-preparation Gate, not an earlier motivating measurement. It explicitly permits a valid Reconcile to reach Bias with opposed components. It does not guarantee an overlap window or prescribe carrying early positive measurements forward. See [PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md) for implementation, tests and history. This report does not call the observed non-overlap a bug, claim global inevitability, or weaken any rule.

Across **114 post-action/initial boundaries × 3 participants = 342 current states**, there are **zero four-way overlaps**. The replay includes the 33-action prefix and all 80 preserved continuation actions, with no ordinary Commit or Emergence transition.
