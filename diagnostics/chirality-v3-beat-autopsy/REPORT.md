# Is the beat an Attestation act or an occupied entry position?

## Outcome

The existing v3 diagnostic explicitly observes **consecutive individual acts**. Its zeros are genuine observations of no represented Cycle entry between acts, not duplicate records or elapsed-time errors. The alternative occupied-position stream measures a different fact and changes the resulting directions materially.

At the same final grounded Bias source, A gives Cadence **−0.3445034 / Negative** and B gives **+0.2908717 / Positive**. The real local Bias-source eligibility calculation changes from false to true under B. Nevertheless, B's existing-source Tension is **−0.0340040**, still negative. This is not an Emergence witness, and no counterfactual Bias write or Commit was executed.

The source supports act multiplicity explicitly in canonical v2's *rate* and in the provisional v3 act-interval diagnostic. It does not establish that this transfer of multiplicity into developmental interval CV is the desired eventual canonical observable. No inspected source establishes occupied positions as the intended beat.

## Coordinate definition and every zero

For the particular observed DomainLineage, let `position(act)` be the number of represented Cycle-entry events preceding the act in the diagnostic's captured event order. Then A's interval is the difference between successive act positions. This is a cumulative structural coordinate, **not the act's owning Cycle**, a participant's Cycle sequence, a timestamp, or a single unique Cycle row.

The final source's 17 acts occupy:

3, 8, 13,13,13,13,13,13, 15,15,15,15, 16,16,16,16,16

## Multiplicity sensitivity

The current provisional stream is sensitive to multiplicity. Replacing one middle-position act by five genuine acts introduces four zero gaps. For a purely mathematical example with occupied coordinates `[0,5,10]`:

| Act occupancy at the three coordinates | A intervals | A CV | A developmental Cadence | B intervals / Cadence |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| [1,1,1] | [5,5] | 0 | +1 | [5,5] / +1 |
| [1,5,1] | [5,0,0,0,0,5] | 1.4142135624 | −0.1715729 | [5,5] / +1 |

This is a read-only arithmetic illustration, not a newly authored fixture. It is **not five times more irregular**: CV and the developmental transform respond nonlinearly. Multiplicity can change maturity, mean, relative dispersion and direction. Under B, extra acts at an already occupied coordinate leave the interval stream unchanged.

Production v2 would count five acts instead of one in its numerator, but its final similarity depends on the previous Domain's rate; it does not label that change “five times more irregular.” Its tests support preserving act count, not this particular interval-CV interpretation. No inspected source supplies a normative yes/no answer that five same-position acts *should* cause a particular amount of rhythmic degradation in a promoted developmental observable.

## Classification

**A for the observable actually defined in the existing diagnostic; C for its unresolved canonical promotion.** Act counting is explicit in production's rate semantics, and individual acts/zero-entry bursts are explicit in the v3 diagnostic lineage. It would be inaccurate to call A merely a hidden implementation accident. However, the source does not establish that carrying that sensitivity into developmental interval CV is the intended final semantic choice.

**B is not established as source intent.** Its cleaner response to co-located acts and the removal of the final direction blocker are diagnostic consequences, not evidence that it is the canonical answer. Nor does its final field cross positive.

The narrow conclusion is: **the current beat is the act, and those acts can genuinely share one represented-entry coordinate. A positional beat is a different, well-defined observable that the existing tests/specifications have not selected.** No selection is made in this investigation.
