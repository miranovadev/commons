# Direct Tragedy and Resolution

The normative lifecycle is [Tragedy, Resolution and recursive Exhaustion](tragedy-resolution-target.md).
The former first/later fresh-birth rule is replaced, not retained as an alternate authority.

An authorised direct expressive birth, Attestation, Instrument or Traverse
encounters its actual target after the action's measurement and evidence changes.
Canonical published Charge zero establishes an immutable terminal encounter.
The action need not lower Charge. Passive Cycle updates, reads, Rest and
projection maintenance do not publish encounters.

The first encounter creates the Region's unique Tragedy and Receipt. The Region
enters Resolution: Variant and Revise are prohibited throughout that Region.
Other ordinary eligible operations remain available on non-terminal continuations.
A stale plan cannot bypass transactional generation checks. Later encounters
terminate additional routes without another Tragedy or Receipt.

Under the same Substrate contribution lock, remaining actual continuations are
evaluated and a unique regional Exhaustion proof is published if none survive.
A single-route Region can exhaust at its first Tragedy. Receipt consumption,
Potential and temporary evidence absence do not prove exhaustion.

The Receipt retains one eligible foreign homologous Bias escape from its owning
unresolved Region. It remains excluded from ordinary evidence, Instrument
Receipts, Reinforcement and movement authority. An unused warrant cannot keep
an otherwise terminal Region alive.

See [implementation and validation](tragedy-resolution-implementation.md),
[regional proofs](commit-region-exhaustion.md) and [recovery](recovery-basins.md).
