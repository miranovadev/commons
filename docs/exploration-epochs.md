# Historical Exploration Epoch model

Structural Exploration Epochs and shared spentness were removed by Stage B on 25 October 2026. They are not current action or recovery authority.

See [Participant-relative exploration](participant-relative-exploration.md) for the replacement contract: immutable personal Rest encounters, active reachable heads, lawful progression and personal Variant eligibility. Deployment uses a fresh database; no epoch migration or backfill is needed. Tragedy, Resolution and regional Exhaustion remain independent.
