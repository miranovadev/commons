# Commit Region Exhaustion

The [approved Stage 4 target](tragedy-resolution-target.md) is normative.
A Region is owned by immutable creating-Cycle entry, not by arbitrary graph
membership. `commit_region_exhaustions` is the unique regional proof authority;
`exhausted_region_memberships` is only its rebuildable ownership projection.

`terminal_encounters` records the actual target, owning Region, encountering
Phase/participant/action, resolved field and version, and event time. The
encountering Phase is not necessarily the target's creating Phase. One target's
terminal event cannot be replaced by a later observation. `tragedies` identifies
the first regional encounter and unique Receipt.

Exhaustion records its direct encounter or exhausted-descendant cause and the
locked continuation evaluation. Successful Commit edges preserve continuing
descendant basins. A surviving sibling prevents closure of a shared ancestor.
Foreign causal operands do not themselves grant an operational continuation.
Recursive publication follows actual Commit sources to their owning Regions.
An ancestor still in Exploration retains ordinary generation permissions;
child Exhaustion cannot fabricate a regional Tragedy or impose Resolution.
An ancestor already in Resolution closes only when its final route terminates.

Publication, derived membership, field invalidation and recursion share the
contribution transaction. PostgreSQL and SQLite reject changes to immutable
encounter, Tragedy and Exhaustion rows. Rollback removes the whole unpublished
event. Historical measurements and canonical Committals remain unchanged.

Fresh installation is the deployment contract. No previous experimental database
is converted, backfilled or interpreted under this rule. Migration validation
must exercise an empty PostgreSQL and SQLite database.

Exhaustion excludes the governing Committal from ordinary entry and traversal.
Recovery keeps the participant's actual recorded backward path and existing
Horizon-boundary crossing authority; a proof cannot create a new movement edge.
