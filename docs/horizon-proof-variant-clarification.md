# Variant eligibility in the Horizon proof stack

This clarification is normative for Distinguish (Phase 1) and Disperse (Phase 4).
Mediate still requires a second proposition to reconcile. No new action or
participant-count requirement is introduced.

The structural position includes its actual source Gate, not all Propositions
with the same phase/register or Commit Region. Further generation must create
an authorised causal continuation from that Gate. Rest recovery returns through
recorded ancestry backward; an epoch advance or greater Horizon Potential never
permits a sideways move. Calculation populations, visibility and action targets
remain distinct. No edge means no movement.

An empty viable structural position exposes Variant. Once a Variant exists,
it must have genuine ordinary evidence (shared catalogue or retained causal
evidence) and at least one effective Attestation. Only when those
prerequisites have been met, every current route is proven spent in this
position's current exploration epoch does another Variant become an available
option. Potential Charge is not a permission condition, including at zero. A viable route
blocks redundant Variant generation. See [exploration epochs](exploration-epochs.md).

Successful paths into an explicitly exhausted child Horizon are resolved
history, not failed options. They are excluded from the viable population
without inventing failure rows. This propagates to a shared expressive source
only while all other outgoing continuations are also resolved or currently
failed; a live alternative prevents it. Their historical numerical gain does
not establish remaining capacity. Ordinary epoch-scoped failures remain separate.

Each new Variant repeats that sequence. Stage 2A abolishes the exact-Write
Instrument prerequisite: shared ordinary evidence can support later participation.
Instrumentation occurs only in Persist, targets its current Gate, does not
advance the Phase, and allows one completed execution per Participant Cycle.
Receipts retain their existing Region/Horizon identity and any eligible
Participant may consume them through Reclassify. This is not Stage 2B's future
Receipt-governed generation policy.

Receipt generation or consumption alone never reopens Variant. No prior
Mediate Rest is required, and there is no fixed two-sibling limit. One
Participant can reach Mediate without a counterpart, Rest/recover, and create
another Variant when it returns, provided these prerequisites hold. Healthy
existing continuations retain their ordinary Attest/Traverse options.

The backend checks every currently viable direct opportunity at the same
producing Phase/register position before allowing another. Failed opportunities
remain visible historical evidence. Failure only blocks actions when its Horizon,
producing structural position and epoch match the current state. A successful
Variant advances only that position's epoch, reopening older failures under the
normal action rules without clearing them. An empty viable position can
start an attempt even at zero currently instantiated Potential; this preserves
the distinction between Potential and proof of Exhaustion.

Potential Charge describes demonstrated reduction in absolute Tension. It does
not decide whether another expressive move is structurally possible, and an
expressive move may increase Tension. Its algebra remains unchanged. The action
schema and contribution dispatcher share the same structural eligibility service.

The former positive-Potential prerequisite and special Tragedy final-attempt
allowance are retired. The allowance service and live bookkeeping are removed;
its table remains historical storage only. Tragedy is now born from the first
fresh expressive result at zero canonical Charge, never from Potential or Rest.
Receipt scope and the legacy exhaustion/recovery rules are retained pending their
later correction stages. See [Charge-boundary Tragedy](branch-tragedy.md).

Tests: `ExpressiveEvidenceGatingTest` covers both expressive phases, positive
and zero Potential, evidence-only and Attestation-only failures, repetition
beyond two Variants, same-Participant creation, shared-Receipt eligibility,
consumption independence, and the existing next-Cycle Traverse rule.
