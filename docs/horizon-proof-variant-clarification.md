# Variant eligibility

[Participant-relative exploration](participant-relative-exploration.md) is the current exploration authority. Shared spentness and structural Exploration Epochs have been removed.

Discover existing lawful non-generative continuations first. A fresh Variant is eligible only when the acting Participant has explored every relevant reachable active head, and the ordinary Phase, structure, evidence and lifecycle prerequisites hold. Fresh Variant is excluded from its own prerequisite frontier. Another Participant's incomplete exploration does not block the actor's earned alternative.

Attestation direction changes remain lawful. Reversal alone does not renew exploration or permanently prevent generation. A new reachable head or newly lawful advancing action releases the corresponding personal restriction. Events remain historical facts; a fresh Variant does not reset old history.

Distinguish and Disperse retain their applicable ordinary evidence and effective Attestation requirements. Persist and Disperse generation retain the unconsumed ordinary Receipt prerequisite, including the existing prepared contribution boundary. Instrument and Preparation allowances, Receipt consumption and authority are unchanged. Potential Charge is not generation permission, including at zero.

Reachability follows exact producing positions and the recorded arrival path within the authorized Region and Horizon. Comparison populations and foreign Reconcile operands do not grant movement. Recovery retains its established backward geometry. Tragedy, Resolution, Commit and regional Exhaustion remain independent.

`ParticipantExplorationTest` and `ParticipantExplorationHttpTest` exercise real personal Rest, reopening and Variant ordering. `ExpressiveEvidenceGatingTest` isolates the unchanged evidence/Attestation/Potential prerequisites from exploration selection. PostgreSQL concurrency tests recheck the shared frontier under the existing Substrate lock.
