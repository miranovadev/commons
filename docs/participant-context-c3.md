## Current selection contract

The same catalogue serves human and autonomous participation. It starts
with observations recorded at the current Gate and continues backward
through the local causal lineage. A Commit carries the actual committed
lineage of the immediately preceding Region. Distinguish at an Origin
therefore shows the Origin's own evidence. Participants can see available
Write statements and gain access to a Write's own observations when they
arrive there. Both branches of a local Reconciliation contribute qualifying
ancestors. A foreign operand does not seed a foreign catalogue.

A personally loaded observation supplies inline content when its origin
is in scope. An observation consumed by a selected Read can also supply
contextual content within that scope. Tragedy keeps its special memory
authority. Ordinary Proposition Search results follow their observation's
scope. Search results grant no movement authority.

The cognitive horizon also supplies the observation origins used to reopen
personal judgment. Operational grounding, consumption, recovery and movement
retain their own selectors. For field definitions and ordering, see the
[Gate-causal Receipt contract](gate-causal-receipt-cognition.md).
